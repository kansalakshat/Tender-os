About Tenderleo

Great opportunities shouldn’t be lost in a PDF.

Every day, thousands of government tenders are published. Some are worth lakhs. Some are worth crores. And many of them are a perfect fit for businesses that never even discover them.

Why?

Because finding the right tender is difficult. Understanding hundreds of pages is exhausting. Checking eligibility takes time. And preparing the right documents can turn a promising opportunity into a missed deadline.

We think it shouldn't be this hard. That’s why we built Tenderleo.

Tenderleo is an AI-powered tender intelligence platform built to help businesses discover, understand, and act on the opportunities that matter to them.

Instead of spending hours jumping between portals, downloading PDFs, searching for eligibility criteria, and figuring out which documents are required, Tenderleo brings the process together in one intelligent workflow.

  • Find the right opportunities.
  • Know whether you qualify.
  • Understand what’s required.
  • Prepare faster.

Built for the businesses behind India’s growth

Contractors. Manufacturers. Service providers. MSMEs.

These businesses already have the experience, capabilities, and people to execute large projects. What they often don't have is the time and resources to constantly monitor thousands of tenders and decode complicated bidding requirements.

Tenderleo bridges that gap. We use AI to reduce the complexity between “A tender was published” and “We are ready to bid.”

Our vision

We envision a world where businesses don't miss opportunities because they couldn't find them, didn't understand them, or couldn't manage the paperwork in time.

Where a small business can discover the same opportunities as a large enterprise.

Where tendering becomes less about navigating complexity, and more about competing on capability.

Because the next big opportunity for your business could already be out there. Tenderleo helps you find it.
