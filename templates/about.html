Questions people ask

Is Tenderleo free?{{ icon('caret-down') }}

Browsing every tender is free, with no account. A free account adds ranked matches, saved tenders and bid tracking.

Where does the data come from?{{ icon('caret-down') }}

Only from official portals such as GeM, CPPP and the state eProcurement sites. Every tender links to its source.

How often is it updated?{{ icon('caret-down') }}

New tenders are collected every day, and a tender leaves the open list on its closing date.

Do you submit bids for me?{{ icon('caret-down') }}

No. You bid on the official portal yourself. Tenderleo helps you find the right tenders and prepare for them.

Is my company’s data safe?{{ icon('caret-down') }}

Your answers are used only to rank tenders for you, and are never shared. You can edit them any time.
