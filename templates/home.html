How Tenderleo works

From thousands of tenders to the right opportunities, automatically

Tendering shouldn't mean spending hours searching portals, reading endless PDFs, and figuring out whether you qualify. Tenderleo simplifies the entire journey.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Find tenders that actually matter to your business.

    Tenderleo scans and organizes tender opportunities from relevant sources, helping you discover projects based on your business, industry, location, capabilities, and experience.

    No more endless searching. No more missed opportunities.

  2. 02

    Analyse

    Let AI read the tender for you.

    Open a tender and Tenderleo breaks down the important information: scope of work, eligibility criteria, deadlines, EMD, experience requirements, turnover, documents, and other key conditions.

    Complex tender documents become clear, actionable information.

  3. 03

    Qualify

    Know whether you should bid, before you spend hours preparing.

    Tenderleo compares the tender requirements with your business profile to help identify whether you meet the key eligibility criteria. Get a clear picture of:

    • Eligibility
    • Missing documents
    • Experience requirements
    • Financial requirements
    • Critical tender conditions

    Bid smarter, not blindly.

  4. 04

    Prepare

    Turn an opportunity into a bid-ready package.

    Once you've decided to bid, Tenderleo helps organize the required information and documents and keeps your bid workflow in one place.

    Less paperwork. Fewer missed requirements. More time to focus on winning the project.

Discover. Analyse. Qualify. Prepare. One intelligent workflow for the entire tender journey.
